Health Disclaimer & Privacy

Health and fitness disclaimer

Luna Training provides general educational fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Training plans, calorie estimates, and macro targets are approximate and not guaranteed to be appropriate for everyone.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new exercise or dietary program, particularly if you have medical conditions, injuries, are pregnant, or take medication. Stop exercising and seek appropriate care if you develop chest pain, faintness, or concerning symptoms.

Users are responsible for evaluating activities and goals in light of their own circumstances. Nothing here excludes legal rights or liabilities that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Privacy notice — current local version

This version stores profile, workout, weight, and nutrition information in your browser's local storage. It does not include cloud accounts or an advertising SDK. Clearing site data may delete records. Use Backup Data to save a copy. Do not enter information you do not want stored on this device.

Before public release, Luna Training needs a full privacy policy and terms of service reflecting its actual hosting, analytics, advertising, and data-handling practices.
